Senior Living Data & Outreach Proposal

A two-phase intelligence pipeline that scrapes and enriches senior living facility data from multiple sources, then uses AI to generate hyper-personalized marketing emails tailored to each facility's gaps and opportunities.

June 8, 2026

The Refiniti Approach

We build a data-first outreach engine for senior living facilities. Phase 1 collects foundational firmographic and contact data from APIs, directories, and scraping tools. Phase 2 enriches each record with SEO scores, tech stack, social presence, and verification status.

An AI interpretation layer reads every enriched field and writes a personalized email body — referencing their specific website issues, missing social profiles, low domain authority, or outdated tech — so every outreach feels hand-crafted, not templated.

Two-Phase Data Pipeline

Data collection is split into two phases so outreach can begin quickly while enrichment runs in parallel.

Rapidly build a working prospect list with core firmographic and contact fields. Enough to start segmented outreach within days.

  • company_name — facility legal and trade names
  • employee_count — staff size estimate
  • annual_revenue — revenue band or estimated range
  • founded_year — year established
  • primary_email — decision-maker or general contact email
  • primary_phone_number — main facility line or admin desk
  • website_url — primary domain

Layer on digital presence, technical, and SEO intelligence to power deep personalization.

  • linkedin_url — company LinkedIn profile
  • facebook_url / instagram_url — social profiles
  • tech_stack — CMS, analytics, chat widgets, booking tools detected on site
  • verification_status — email/phone deliverability and validity score
  • seo_score — on-page and technical SEO health (0–100)
  • keywords_ranking_for — top organic keywords and positions
  • domain_authority — Moz/Ahrefs-style authority score

Data Sources & Collection Methods

We combine API integrations, licensed data providers, and targeted scraping to build a complete facility profile.

  • Apollo.io / ZoomInfo / Clearbit — firmographics, employee count, revenue, contact emails
  • Google Places API — facility name, phone, address, website, reviews
  • CMS / State Licensing Databases — licensed senior care facilities by state
  • NeverBounce / ZeroBounce — email verification and deliverability scoring
  • Ahrefs / SEMrush / Moz API — SEO score, domain authority, keyword rankings
  • BuiltWith / Wappalyzer API — tech stack detection
  • Apify / Bright Data — scrape senior living directories (Caring.com, A Place for Mom, SeniorAdvisor)
  • Custom web scrapers — facility websites for contact pages, social links, and on-page signals
  • Social profile discovery — LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram URL matching by company name + domain
  • n8n / Make.com orchestration — automated pipeline chaining scrape ? enrich ? verify ? store

AI Personalization Engine

For every enriched record, AI reads all fields and generates a contextual note plus a personalized email body. Each data point maps to a specific outreach angle.

Data Field AI Interpretation Outreach Angle
seo_score (low) "Site scores 34/100 — missing meta descriptions, slow load time, no schema markup." Offer SEO audit + on-page fixes to improve local search visibility for "senior living near [city]."
domain_authority (low) "DA 18 — limited backlinks; competitors in area average DA 35+." Propose content marketing and local citation building to close the authority gap.
keywords_ranking_for "Ranks #14 for 'assisted living [city]' — page 2, below click threshold." Highlight specific keyword opportunity and projected traffic lift from page 2 ? page 1.
tech_stack (outdated) "Running WordPress 5.4 (unsupported), no SSL on contact form page." Recommend website modernization — security, mobile UX, and lead capture upgrades.
facebook_url / instagram_url (missing) "No active Facebook or Instagram presence detected — families research facilities on social first." Offer social media setup + content calendar tailored to family decision-makers.
employee_count + annual_revenue "45 employees, ~$3.2M revenue — mid-size independent operator, likely budget-conscious." Route to the correct revenue segment and apply the matching discounted service tier.
verification_status (invalid email) "Primary email bounced — fallback to phone outreach or alternate contact discovery." Auto-route to phone/SMS sequence instead of email; flag for re-enrichment.
founded_year (established) "Operating since 1987 — 38 years of community trust, but digital presence hasn't kept pace." Lead with respect for their legacy, then bridge to modern digital gaps.
